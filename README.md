## 🌟 Overview / ภาพรวมม็อด

**Frieren Flight** is a multi-loader addon for **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks**, faithfully introducing legendary spells from *Sousou no Frieren (Frieren: Beyond Journey's End)* along with Megumin's iconic Explosion magic from *KonoSuba*.

Available for both **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)** and **Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge)**.

**Frieren Flight** เป็นม็อด Addon เสริมสำหรับ **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks** ที่เพิ่มเวทมนตร์ระดับตำนานจากอนิเมะ *คำอธิษฐานในวันที่จากลา (Frieren)* และสุดยอดเวทมนตร์ระเบิดกัมปนาทของเมกุมินจาก *KonoSuba* พร้อมเอฟเฟกต์ 3D VFX และระบบเสียงรอบทิศทางเต็มรูปแบบ

## ✨ Featured Spells / รายละเอียดเวทมนตร์

### 1. 🪽 Flight Magic (人類の魔法 - เวทมนตร์บินได้)
- **School:** Ender
- **Rarity:** Rare (Levels 1–5)
- **Cast Type:** Instant (Toggle On/Off)
- **Mechanics:**
  - Grants unrestricted 3D flight with a subtle glowing magic aura under your feet.
  - Dynamically consumes mana continuously while flying (18 mana/sec at Level 1, scaling down to 6 mana/sec at Level 5).
  - **Cinematic Ascension:** Triggers a cinematic third-person camera lift with ease-in-out curve, subtle FOV pulse, and smooth hover settle. Includes ceiling collision detection to prevent clipping into roofs or caves.
  - **Fail-Safe Descent:** If mana is depleted mid-air, the spell automatically triggers a safe 20-second *Slow Falling* descent to prevent fatal fall damage. Completely cancels fall damage upon landing.

---

### 2. 🗡️ Reelseiden (レイルザイデン - เวทตัดสะบั้น)
- **School:** Evocation
- **Rarity:** Epic (Levels 1–5)
- **Cast Type:** Instant
- **Mechanics:**
  - The iconic invisible cleaving magic of Übel.
  - Unleashes an instantaneous supersonic crescent blade that cuts through the atmosphere in your direct line of sight.
  - **100% True Armor Piercing:** Completely bypasses physical armor, magic defenses, and natural protections.
  - **Shield Breaker:** Immediately slices through and disables opponent shields.

---

### 3. ⚡ Judradjim (ジュドラジム - เวทสายฟ้าทำลายล้าง)
- **School:** Lightning
- **Rarity:** Legendary (Levels 1–5)
- **Cast Type:** Long Cast (1.5s Channel)
- **Mechanics:**
  - The cataclysmic thunder magic of Master Denken.
  - Calls down a colossal 38-meter black and gold lightning pillar from the heavens upon the target location or terrain.
  - Deals devastating direct and area damage (60–110+ damage) while emitting an explosive outward shockwave that violently launches surrounding enemies.

---

### 4. 🌸 Flower Bed Magic (花畑を出す魔法 - เวทเสกทุ่งดอกไม้)
- **School:** Nature
- **Rarity:** Epic (Levels 1–5)
- **Cast Type:** Long Cast (1.0s Serene Channel)
- **Mechanics:**
  - Frieren & Himmel's iconic flower garden spell.
  - Conjures an expansive, picturesque meadow across a **64.0-block radius**, expanding outward in a sequential 35-tick blooming ripple wave.
  - Features dense clusters of the legendary **Blue Moonweed** (*Cornflower & Blue Orchid*), white bells (*Lily of the Valley*), and verdant moss carpets on stone surfaces.
  - **Visuals:** Ambient translucent sunbeams (God Rays) and 3D fluttering petals drifting naturally with the breeze.
  - **Sanctuary Buff (Flower Blessing):**
    - Rapid Health Regeneration: **+5.0 to +10.0 HP/sec**
    - Rapid Mana Recovery: **+15.0 to +29.0 Mana/sec**
    - **50% Damage Reduction** against all incoming hostile damage.
  - **Hostile Decay:** Drains **-2% Max HP every second** from all hostile monsters within the 64-block territory!

---

### 5. 💥 Explosion Magic (エクスプロージョン - เวทระเบิดกัมปนาท)
- **School:** Fire
- **Rarity:** Legendary (Levels 1–5)
- **Cast Type:** Long Cast (4.5s Incantation)
- **Mechanics:**
  - Megumin's signature apocalyptic detonation magic.
  - **Chanting Phase:** The caster is firmly rooted in place while rotating red-and-gold runic seals appear underfoot and an authentic 110-meter sky pillar ascends into the stratosphere.
  - **Detonation Phase:** A stratospheric rainbow star flare descends into a high-density plasma beam strike, culminating in a megaton 4-point cross star blast and a colossal 50-meter 3D mushroom cloud.
  - **Destruction:** Inflicts **850–1,150+ cataclysmic damage**, carves a deep 28-block crater into terrain, and sets surrounding rims ablaze.
  - **Lore Accuracy:** Burns **100% of remaining mana** to 0 and inflicts Megumin's collapse fatigue (Slowness & Weakness for 12 seconds).

## 🚀 Installation / วิธีการติดตั้ง

1. Install **Minecraft Forge** (for 1.20.1) or **NeoForge** (for 1.21.1).
2. Download and install **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks** and its respective dependencies (e.g., Curios / PlayerAnimator).
3. Download the corresponding `frieren_flight` jar file from [Releases](https://github.com/ling-gwdgw2/Frieren-Flight---Iron-s-Spells-Addon/releases):
   - For 1.21.1: `frieren_flight-neoforge-1.21.1-1.0.0.jar`
   - For 1.20.1: `frieren_flight-forge-1.20.1-1.0.0.jar`
4. Place the `.jar` into your Minecraft `.minecraft/mods` directory.
5. Launch the game and enjoy!

## 🛠️ Building from Source / การคอมไพล์จาก Source Code

The project uses a high-performance multi-loader build script:

```bash
# Build both Forge 1.20.1 and NeoForge 1.21.1 targets
python build_multiloader.py --target all

# Or build a specific target:
python build_multiloader.py --target neoforge
python build_multiloader.py --target forge
```

Output JARs will be generated in the root directory:
- `frieren_flight-neoforge-1.21.1-1.0.0.jar`
- `frieren_flight-forge-1.20.1-1.0.0.jar`
